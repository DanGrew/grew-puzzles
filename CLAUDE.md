# grew-puzzles — root index

The **public** site: Grew Puzzles, free puzzles with no ads and no paywalls; signing in, with
Google through Supabase, is optional and exists only to save progress. Served
as a static site from GitHub Pages at `https://dangrew.github.io/grew-puzzles/` — **no build
step**. Every generator and construction rule lives in the private `grew-puzzles-tooling` repo,
not here.

## ⛔ The site is dumb

| repo | visibility | holds |
|---|---|---|
| `grew-puzzles` (this one) | **public** | the pages, every puzzle file — each one already generated and validated — and the sign-in and save code with Supabase's public key |
| `grew-puzzles-tooling` | **private** | the generator, the word groups, every placement and construction rule, the puzzle format and its checks, and the Supabase database setup and its rules (its `docs/DATABASE.md`) |

- ⛔ This repo holds **puzzles and pages, nothing else**. No generation, word-group or placement logic ever ships here, not even as page code — a page only plays a puzzle that already exists.
- ⛔ Supabase's secret key never comes here — only its address and public (publishable) key, in `ui/sign-in-ui.js`, beside Google's client ID, public too. The database's own rules are what keep each player's progress theirs.
- No puzzle format, schema or check lives here either: they're all in tooling. This repo's local checks run tooling's `origin/main` checks over `content/` (`scripts/check-puzzles.sh`); the format is tooling's `docs/PUZZLE-FORMAT.md`.

## Before you implement

- **Work in a worktree off `origin/main`** — never branch-switch the primary checkout.

Process rules are **claude-workflow**'s, not this repo's:

- The product entry — ask `claude-config/bin/grew-product-dir grew-puzzles` where it lives, never assemble the path — holds the board, the specs and this product's deltas.

## The shape of the site

GitHub Pages serves `main` root as-is — a merge to `main` is the deploy.

| path | what it is |
|---|---|
| `index.html` | root redirect to `app/` |
| `app/index.html` | the landing page — the browse grid of one place: a kind — Wordsearches, where it opens, or Mazes — each puzzle of it once, or Collections, each collection as a tile of its own (name, description, type breakdown), whatever its puzzles' kinds; the side bar, or on a phone the Wordsearches \| Mazes switch above the grid, goes to another. Each puzzle tile shows its kind's picture (`ui/kind-picture-ui.js`) between its strip and its title, its strip in the puzzle's own difficulty — a maze's the one the owner saved in its index entry, else its type's, as every wordsearch's is — and a maze's tile reads its size, width×height, under its type once its entry has one. The place's tiles are filtered from a Filters popup, the same for every kind — a Difficulty row, Easy to Extreme, each in its colour, the difficulties the place's puzzles have; a Type row, the kind's types in one list; picks in a row widen, the two rows narrow each other — and — signed in — a Finished row last, Finished or Not finished, one at a time, narrowing whatever the types show to the tiles with a ✓ or without one; a place with nothing to filter, Collections signed out, has no Filters button — and sorted by difficulty, date, title or type (by default difficulty, easiest first: the filter rows' order, each difficulty by type then title A to Z; flipped, hardest first), the place, filter and sort held in the address (never whether the popup is open) (`?kind=maze|collections&type=…&difficulty=…&finished=yes|no&sort=…&dir=…` — no kind for Wordsearches, a type always its kind's; a Finished choice is dropped whenever nobody is signed in, and with one in the address the grid draws nothing until the progress arrives), read from `content/puzzles/wordsearch/index.json`, `content/puzzles/maze/index.json` (empty until the first maze, as tooling's Save starts a kind) and `content/collections/index.json`, never a puzzle file — a maze's tile opens `maze.html`, a wordsearch's `play.html` — bar the ticks and the rail: signed in, each tile whose puzzles the player has finished wears a ✓, a collection's once every puzzle in it is, and a Continue playing rail above the filters holds the puzzles started and not finished, most recently played first, as many browse tiles as fit across with ‹ › between sets — untouched by the filters and sort, and gone signed out or with nothing in play; both arrive after the tiles draw |
| `app/collection.html` | a collection page — `collection.html?slug=<slug>`: its name and description, Print book, then its puzzles, whatever their kind, as tiles in number order, each marked with its number, and — signed in — a ✓ on each one finished; no filter or sort |
| `app/book.html` | a collection's book — `book.html?slug=<slug>`, opened by Print book: the book is its own KDP interior, every page 8.5×11 in with no margin, so no browser header or footer prints, each page keeping its own 0.5 in edges: a title page (name, description, the site's address, where the answers are), the copyright page in the owner's words (the product's `docs/CONTENT.md`), then each wordsearch's own printout in number order — a maze is played on the site alone, and left out — each of its pages headed Puzzle 1, Puzzle 2… — one page, or for a puzzle of several grids its words then a page per grid — and each puzzle starting a fresh page; puzzle pages are numbered at the foot on the outer side, on from the title page's 1, so even numbers fall on the left. On screen, under Print book, a one-line hint for saving it as a PDF from Chrome. The print dialog opens once every page is drawn. Each page is a copy of `play.html`'s own markup drawn by its `drawSheet`, so the printout and the book are one layout |
| `app/play.html` | the play page — `play.html?id=<hidden ID>`, which opens `content/puzzles/wordsearch/<hidden ID>.json`; the play URL in tooling's `docs/PUZZLE-FORMAT.md`. A puzzle of several grids (a Saga) shows them under tabs over its one word list, and prints its words on a sheet of their own, across the page whichever way they sit on screen, then each grid on its own. On paper a word with several copies reads `Sheep ×45` — every copy, never the screen's progress — and the words' columns are as wide as the list's widest word, a count kept on its word, 88px at the least, so no word runs into the next; a longer line of several words wraps at a space. Where the words sit and the text size (Tiny to Huge, scaling the grid's letters and the words together, on screen only) are the player's, kept on the device. Signed in, every find is saved as a line as it's made, and the puzzle opens with them already drawn; signing in mid-puzzle syncs that puzzle — its finds on screen saved, its finds saved elsewhere joining the board; signed out, nothing is saved and a quiet slip under the words invites the player to sign in, its Sign in opening the site bar's card. A listed word with an entry — found through the puzzle's `wordGroups` in `content/entries/index.json`, the format tooling's `docs/PUZZLE-FORMAT.md` — wears a dotted underline, and tapping it, found or not, opens its popup beside it (on a phone, a card across the foot of the screen): the word, its definition, a living thing's five facts, the credit and Read more on Wikipedia, each where the entry has it; Esc, its ✕ or a tap anywhere else closes it, that tap landing on nothing, and reading never touches the board. No entries file, no marks; the printout never shows either |
| `app/maze.html` | the maze play page — `maze.html?id=<hidden ID>`, which opens `content/puzzles/maze/<hidden ID>.json`: the maze in the grid card, sized to the window's height, under its type band, and a Checklist card where a wordsearch's words would be — Guides, Keys and Collectibles counted, A, B, C, then the six exits in the order ABC to CBA, or End in a maze without letters, each line as the maze has it. The player taps a trail from the highlighted start, one open cell at a time; tapping an earlier cell of the trail backs out to it. Stepping on a guide, collectible, key or letter picks it up — it fades, its line ticks, and stays ticked whatever the trail does; a zone whose key isn't held refuses the step and flashes its key's number; stepping on an exit marks it, the right one ✓ and a wrong one ✗. Every line ticked pops and sparkles as a finished wordsearch does. The flip shows the saved solution: the main path blue, the detours green, the right exit dotted, the wrong ones crossed — read off the file's walk, never worked out. Themed, collectibles and blocks wear the puzzle's character; Plain, a dot and a solid tile. A control pad — up, down, left, right round Back, beside the checklist, under the grid card on a phone, and the arrow keys and Backspace — runs the trail to the next junction, dead end or anything on the checklist, never past one, so it solves nothing; a way with a wall greys out, a locked zone stops a run at its edge, and Back, or the way the trail came, goes back one move. The grid card is a frame: every move keeps the player in its centre; − and + (or a pinch, or Ctrl-wheel) zoom from the whole maze to 48px cells, a maze too big to tap whole opening zoomed in on its start; zoomed in, a little map in its top-right corner shows the whole maze, the view's box, the trail and every collectible, key, letter and exit, its box dragged to look round. Only the cells round the view are drawn, so any size plays. Nothing is saved yet, nor printed, and a collection's book leaves its mazes out |
| `app/about.html` | the About us page — the owner's words (the product's `docs/CONTENT.md`, About us): Thank you!, About us, About these puzzles; reached from About us, every side bar's entry after Saving your progress. Dressed like Privacy, Themed |
| `app/how-to-play.html` | the How to play page — the owner's words (the product's `docs/CONTENT.md`, How to play): the types listed at the top, then a section a type, each at its own address (`how-to-play.html#mirrage`), its picture beside its words, sides alternating, above them on a phone. The pictures are `content/how-to-play/<section>.png`, drawn by tooling's `tools/grew-puzzles-how-to-play` (its `docs/MEDIA.md`), never by hand; a new type is one more section, list entry and picture. Reached from How to play, every side bar's first text page. Dressed like About us |
| `app/saving.html` | the Saving your progress page — the owner's words (the product's `docs/CONTENT.md`, Saving your progress): what signing in does for a player, a short paragraph a point, each led in bold, no picture. Every sentence must stay true of `ui/wordsearch/progress-ui.js` — a change to saving rewords it in the same PR. Reached from Saving your progress, every side bar's entry after How to play. Dressed like About us |
| `app/privacy.html` | the Privacy page — the owner's words (the product's `docs/CONTENT.md`, Privacy), the puzzles' own email a link that starts an email to it; reached from Privacy, the last page in every side bar, before the look. Themed, the words sit on a card (`.page-card`) with a random character and its name beside it, on a random background |
| Themed / Plain | the player's look, picked at the foot of every page's side bar, Themed until they pick Plain, kept in the browser (`components/site-bar.js`). Themed dresses the landing, collection, book, play and text pages in the owner's characters (`content/characters/`): each tile a small figure in its corner, the play page its puzzle's character, background and template, a text page a random one, the book page a random background behind the print dialog, which waits for it up to 1.5 s; `styles/look.css` holds every themed rule, on screen only, so Plain is the site as it was and loads no character image; `core/theme-core.js` decides which character, template and spot, `ui/theme-ui.js` dresses a page's parts. The printout and the book stay plain (TASK-46) |
| `core/` | pure page logic, `*-core.js` ES modules, under the unit and mutation gates — `core/kind-core.js` the kinds' — which kind a puzzle is, each kind's types' difficulty, the one place it is written, each type's side bar dot, a puzzle's own difficulty — the one saved in its index entry, else its type's — a type always with its kind, and the side bar's map of the site — `core/browse-core.js` the browse grid's rules — the rail's sets too — `core/collection-core.js` the collection page's, `core/book-core.js` the collection book's — its page numbers too, `core/wordsearch/play-core.js` the play page's — how far its play area reaches past the column, never over the side bar, too — `core/maze/play-core.js` the maze page's — the trail, the pad's runs and Back, the checklist, the zones' locks, the solution's paths, the view, its zoom, the window of cells drawn and the little map, read off the maze file — `core/entries-core.js` a word's entry's — which listed words have one, what its popup shows and where it sits — `core/wordsearch/progress-core.js` saved progress's — a find is a line (page, start cell, direction), a line saved twice is one find, signing in mid-puzzle syncs it, and when the line under the words shows the invitation or the not-saved note — `core/wordsearch/done-core.js` the ticks' — finished worked out as the play page does, from the saved lines and only the started puzzles' files, which tiles wear a ✓, and which started puzzles are still in play, by their latest found time — `core/day-core.js` how every page shows a created date, `core/auth-core.js` sign-in's — the fingerprint of the one-time word that ties Google's answer to the page, what the bar shows for them, and when Delete my account worked |
| `ui/` | each page's DOM code, ES modules importing `core/` — `ui/side-bar-ui.js` fills every page's side bar from the indexes, `ui/kind-picture-ui.js` draws a tile's kind picture, `ui/wordsearch/play-ui.js` draws and wires the play page, `ui/maze/play-ui.js` the maze page, `ui/card-ui.js` the flip and the completion pop both share, `ui/wordsearch/entry-ui.js` marks its words that have an entry and opens and closes their popup, `ui/wordsearch/progress-ui.js` saves a signed-in player's finds through sign-in's client and reads them back before the grid draws, syncs the puzzle when the player signs in mid-puzzle, trying a failed save again until it lands, `ui/wordsearch/done-ui.js` reads every saved line of a signed-in player's, 1000 rows at a time, opens the files of the puzzles they've started and hands the landing and collection pages the finished ones for their ✓s, and the landing page the ones still in play for its rail — again at every sign-in change, so signing out takes them away, `ui/book-ui.js` puts the collection book together, `ui/sign-in-ui.js` is sign-in in every page's site bar: Sign in at its right (a figure, below 360px wide), opening a card with Google's own button, whose window opens over the page — the player never leaves it, and Supabase is handed Google's token; signed in the player's Google picture — their initial in a circle without one — opening a menu with their email, Sign out and Delete my account — asked once on the site's own card, it calls the database's `delete_account()`, which removes the player and every progress row of theirs, then signs them out where they are with a note that the account is deleted; it holds Supabase's address and public key, and Google's client ID, and its client is the page's one way to Supabase |
| `vendor/supabase.js` | Supabase's library, supabase-js 2.117.2's browser build (`dist/umd/supabase.js`), copied in whole with its MIT licence (`vendor/supabase-js-MIT-LICENSE`, which MIT requires travel with the copy — it covers that file alone; the site itself is all rights reserved, `README.md`) — no page loads code from another site, bar one: Google's sign-in script (`accounts.google.com/gsi/client`), fetched only once a player taps Sign in. Every page loads it, then `ui/sign-in-ui.js`. To update it, copy a newer release's same file over it; the arch checks pass over `vendor/` |
| `content/` | the site's data, apart from its code — the only place JSON lives (`no-json-in-repo`). The puzzles, by kind: `content/puzzles/<kind>/` holds `index.json` (every puzzle's hidden ID, type, created date and title, and a maze's saved difficulty, width and height) and one `<hidden ID>.json` per puzzle. `content/collections/index.json` holds every collection, and `content/entries/index.json` every word entry by word group, both written by tooling's publish, never by hand here — a site with no entries has no `entries/`. The layout is tooling's `docs/PUZZLE-FORMAT.md`. `content/characters/` is the site's own: the owner's characters in their order (`index.json`, its shape `content/schemas/characters.schema.json`), each a cut-out figure (`<name>.webp`) and a background (`<name>-bg.webp`), converted from the owner's PNGs — a new batch is added at the end, never reordered, so collections keep their characters |
| `components/site-bar.js` | the site bar every page shares — fills `<header class="site" data-site-bar data-home data-current>` with the name and sign-in, and puts the side bar, the map of the site, down the page's left, moving the rest of the page into the column beside it (the product's `docs/MOCKUP-KIND-LAYOUT.html`, Side bar with Tabs). `data-current` names the page's place — a kind, `collections`, or a text page; the landing page's is its address. A page's own `[data-menu-entry]` children come first in the side bar (the play page's Print); then Wordsearches and Mazes, each with its count and its types, a dot by each type — a wordsearch type's in its difficulty colour, a maze type's in its own shade, light blue to deep violet, as each maze's difficulty is the owner's call — a type opening its kind filtered to it alone — filled from the indexes by `ui/side-bar-ui.js` — then Collections, shown while a collection exists, How to play, Saving your progress, About us and Privacy, and the look — Themed / Plain — last; the bar sets the look on `<html data-look>` before any page script runs. On a phone the side bar is a drawer over a dimmed page, opened by the burger at the bar's left. Never printed |
