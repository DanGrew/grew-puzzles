# grew-puzzles — root index

The **public** site: Grew Puzzles, free puzzles with no ads and no paywalls; signing in, with
Google through Supabase, is optional and exists only to save progress. Served
as a static site from GitHub Pages at `https://dangrew.github.io/grew-puzzles/` — **no build
step**. Every generator and construction rule lives in the private `grew-puzzles-tooling` repo,
not here.

## ⛔ The site is dumb

| repo | visibility | holds |
|---|---|---|
| `grew-puzzles` (this one) | **public** | the pages, every puzzle file — each one already generated and validated — and the sign-in and save code with Supabase's public key |
| `grew-puzzles-tooling` | **private** | the generator, the word groups, every placement and construction rule, the puzzle format and its checks, and the Supabase database setup and its rules (its `docs/DATABASE.md`) |

- ⛔ This repo holds **puzzles and pages, nothing else**. No generation, word-group or placement logic ever ships here, not even as page code — a page only plays a puzzle that already exists.
- ⛔ Supabase's secret key never comes here — only its address and public (publishable) key, in `ui/sign-in-ui.js`, beside Google's client ID, public too. The database's own rules are what keep each player's progress theirs.
- No puzzle format, schema or check lives here either: they're all in tooling. This repo's local checks run tooling's `origin/main` checks over `content/` (`scripts/check-puzzles.sh`); the format is tooling's `docs/PUZZLE-FORMAT.md`.

## Before you implement

- **Work in a worktree off `origin/main`** — never branch-switch the primary checkout.

Process rules are **claude-workflow**'s, not this repo's:

## The shape of the site

GitHub Pages serves `main` root as-is — a merge to `main` is the deploy.

| path | what it is |
|---|---|
| `index.html` | root redirect to `app/` |
| `app/index.html` | the landing page — the browse grid: every puzzle once, and each collection as a tile of its own (name, description, type breakdown), filtered by type or Collections from a Filters popup — a row per difficulty, Easy to Extreme, a difficulty's name picking its whole row, Collections next, and — signed in — a Finished row last, Finished or Not finished, one at a time, narrowing whatever the types show to the tiles with a ✓ or without one — and sorted by difficulty, date, title or type (by default difficulty, easiest first: the filter rows' order, Easy to Extreme then collections, each difficulty by type then title A to Z; flipped, hardest first), the filter and sort held in the address (never a difficulty, never whether the popup is open) (`?type=…&finished=yes|no&sort=…&dir=…`, `?type=Collections` for collections alone; a Finished choice is dropped whenever nobody is signed in, and with one in the address the grid draws nothing until the progress arrives), read from `content/puzzles/wordsearch/index.json` and `content/collections/index.json`, never a puzzle file — bar the ticks and the rail: signed in, each tile whose puzzles the player has finished wears a ✓, a collection's once every puzzle in it is, and a Continue playing rail above the filters holds the puzzles started and not finished, most recently played first, as many browse tiles as fit across with ‹ › between sets — untouched by the filters and sort, and gone signed out or with nothing in play; both arrive after the tiles draw |
| `app/collection.html` | a collection page — `collection.html?slug=<slug>`: its name and description, Print book, then its puzzles as tiles in number order, each marked with its number, and — signed in — a ✓ on each one finished; no filter or sort |
| `app/book.html` | a collection's book — `book.html?slug=<slug>`, opened by Print book: the book is its own KDP interior, every page 8.5×11 in with no margin, so no browser header or footer prints, each page keeping its own 0.5 in edges: a title page (name, description, the site's address, where the answers are), the copyright page in the owner's words (the product's `docs/CONTENT.md`), then each puzzle's own printout in number order, each of its pages headed Puzzle 1, Puzzle 2… — one page, or for a puzzle of several grids its words then a page per grid — and each puzzle starting a fresh page; puzzle pages are numbered at the foot on the outer side, on from the title page's 1, so even numbers fall on the left. On screen, under Print book, a one-line hint for saving it as a PDF from Chrome. The print dialog opens once every page is drawn. Each page is a copy of `play.html`'s own markup drawn by its `drawSheet`, so the printout and the book are one layout |
| `app/play.html` | the play page — `play.html?id=<hidden ID>`, which opens `content/puzzles/wordsearch/<hidden ID>.json`; the play URL in tooling's `docs/PUZZLE-FORMAT.md`. A puzzle of several grids (a Saga) shows them under tabs over its one word list, and prints its words on a sheet of their own, then each grid on its own. Where the words sit and the text size (Tiny to Huge, scaling the grid's letters and the words together, on screen only) are the player's, kept on the device. Signed in, every find is saved as a line as it's made, and the puzzle opens with them already drawn; signing in mid-puzzle syncs that puzzle — its finds on screen saved, its finds saved elsewhere joining the board; signed out, nothing is saved and a quiet slip under the words invites the player to sign in, its Sign in opening the site bar's card. A listed word with an entry — found through the puzzle's `wordGroups` in `content/entries/index.json`, the format tooling's `docs/PUZZLE-FORMAT.md` — wears a dotted underline, and tapping it, found or not, opens its popup beside it (on a phone, a card across the foot of the screen): the word, its definition, a living thing's five facts, the credit and Read more on Wikipedia, each where the entry has it; Esc, its ✕ or a tap anywhere else closes it, that tap landing on nothing, and reading never touches the board. No entries file, no marks; the printout never shows either |
| `app/about.html` | the About us page — the owner's words (the product's `docs/CONTENT.md`, About us): Thank you!, About us, About these puzzles; reached from About us, every menu's entry after the page's own. Dressed like Privacy, Themed |
| `app/how-to-play.html` | the How to play page — the owner's words (the product's `docs/CONTENT.md`, How to play): the types listed at the top, then a section a type, each at its own address (`how-to-play.html#mirrage`), its picture beside its words, sides alternating, above them on a phone. The pictures are `content/how-to-play/<section>.png`, drawn by tooling's `tools/grew-puzzles-how-to-play` (its `docs/MEDIA.md`), never by hand; a new type is one more section, list entry and picture. Reached from How to play, every menu's entry before About us. Dressed like About us |
| `app/privacy.html` | the Privacy page — the owner's words (the product's `docs/CONTENT.md`, Privacy), the puzzles' own email a link that starts an email to it; reached from Privacy, the last entry in every page's menu. Themed, the words sit on a card (`.page-card`) with a random character and its name beside it, on a random background |
| Themed / Plain | the player's look, picked in every page's menu, Themed until they pick Plain, kept in the browser (`components/site-bar.js`). Themed dresses the landing, collection, book, play and text pages in the owner's characters (`content/characters/`): each tile a small figure in its corner, the play page its puzzle's character, background and template, a text page a random one, the book page a random background behind the print dialog, which waits for it up to 1.5 s; `styles/look.css` holds every themed rule, on screen only, so Plain is the site as it was and loads no character image; `core/theme-core.js` decides which character, template and spot, `ui/theme-ui.js` dresses a page's parts. The printout and the book stay plain (TASK-46) |
| `core/` | pure page logic, `*-core.js` ES modules, under the unit and mutation gates — `core/browse-core.js` is the browse grid's rules — the rail's sets too — and each type's difficulty, the one place it is written, `core/collection-core.js` the collection page's, `core/book-core.js` the collection book's — its page numbers too, `core/wordsearch/play-core.js` the play page's, `core/entries-core.js` a word's entry's — which listed words have one, what its popup shows and where it sits — `core/wordsearch/progress-core.js` saved progress's — a find is a line (page, start cell, direction), a line saved twice is one find, signing in mid-puzzle syncs it, and when the line under the words shows the invitation or the not-saved note — `core/wordsearch/done-core.js` the ticks' — finished worked out as the play page does, from the saved lines and only the started puzzles' files, which tiles wear a ✓, and which started puzzles are still in play, by their latest found time — `core/day-core.js` how every page shows a created date, `core/auth-core.js` sign-in's — the fingerprint of the one-time word that ties Google's answer to the page, what the bar shows for them, and when Delete my account worked |
| `ui/` | each page's DOM code, ES modules importing `core/` — `ui/wordsearch/play-ui.js` draws and wires the play page, `ui/wordsearch/entry-ui.js` marks its words that have an entry and opens and closes their popup, `ui/wordsearch/progress-ui.js` saves a signed-in player's finds through sign-in's client and reads them back before the grid draws, syncs the puzzle when the player signs in mid-puzzle, trying a failed save again until it lands, `ui/wordsearch/done-ui.js` reads every saved line of a signed-in player's, 1000 rows at a time, opens the files of the puzzles they've started and hands the landing and collection pages the finished ones for their ✓s, and the landing page the ones still in play for its rail — again at every sign-in change, so signing out takes them away, `ui/book-ui.js` puts the collection book together, `ui/sign-in-ui.js` is sign-in in every page's site bar: Sign in beside the burger (a figure, below 360px wide), opening a card with Google's own button, whose window opens over the page — the player never leaves it, and Supabase is handed Google's token; signed in the player's Google picture — their initial in a circle without one — opening a menu with their email, Sign out and Delete my account — asked once on the site's own card, it calls the database's `delete_account()`, which removes the player and every progress row of theirs, then signs them out where they are with a note that the account is deleted; it holds Supabase's address and public key, and Google's client ID, and its client is the page's one way to Supabase |
| `vendor/supabase.js` | Supabase's library, supabase-js 2.117.2's browser build (`dist/umd/supabase.js`), copied in whole with its MIT licence (`vendor/supabase-js-MIT-LICENSE`, which MIT requires travel with the copy — it covers that file alone; the site itself is all rights reserved, `README.md`) — no page loads code from another site, bar one: Google's sign-in script (`accounts.google.com/gsi/client`), fetched only once a player taps Sign in. Every page loads it, then `ui/sign-in-ui.js`. To update it, copy a newer release's same file over it; the arch checks pass over `vendor/` |
| `content/` | the site's data, apart from its code — the only place JSON lives (`no-json-in-repo`). The puzzles, by kind: `content/puzzles/<kind>/` holds `index.json` (every puzzle's hidden ID, type, created date and title) and one `<hidden ID>.json` per puzzle. `content/collections/index.json` holds every collection, and `content/entries/index.json` every word entry by word group, both written by tooling's publish, never by hand here — a site with no entries has no `entries/`. The layout is tooling's `docs/PUZZLE-FORMAT.md`. `content/characters/` is the site's own: the owner's characters in their order (`index.json`, its shape `content/schemas/characters.schema.json`), each a cut-out figure (`<name>.webp`) and a background (`<name>-bg.webp`), converted from the owner's PNGs — a new batch is added at the end, never reordered, so collections keep their characters |
| `components/site-bar.js` | the site bar every page shares — fills `<header class="site" data-site-bar data-home data-current>`; a page's own `[data-menu-entry]` children become its menu in place of the site's sections (the play page's Print). Its Collections entry starts hidden; a page that finds a collection shows it. How to play and About us follow the page's own entries and Privacy ends every menu, a page's own included, with the look — Themed / Plain — between them; the bar sets the look on `<html data-look>` before any page script runs |
| `styles/theme.css` · `styles/site-bar.css` · `styles/browse.css` · `styles/play.css` · `styles/book.css` · `styles/page.css` · `styles/look.css` (Themed's) | the Banded tokens (light only), the site bar's look and its sign-in's — Delete my account's question and note too — the browse grid's, its Continue playing rail's, its filter popup's and the collection page's — a finished tile's ✓ too — with each difficulty's colour, a line per difficulty, that tile strips and filter rows both read — the play page's — its `@media print` block is the printout, the one print layout, its grid letters never under 7 pt — the book's page size, title and copyright pages, number headings and page numbers, and a page of the owner's words (About us, Privacy) |
| `tests/fixtures/` | a test puzzle and a test entries file the e2e suite serves in place of real ones — never on the site |
